## 🚀 Key Engineering Projects

### 1. **Super Wideband (3–18 GHz) Conformal Vivaldi Antenna for Electronic Warfare & UAVs**
*Tools: CST Studio Suite 2025, Rogers RT6035HTC, Rogers RT5880, CPW Feed*
- Designed an ultra-compact planar and aerodynamically conformal Vivaldi antenna operating across the 3.0 GHz to 18.0 GHz spectrum ($6:1$ bandwidth ratio with $S_{11} < -10\text{ dB}$).
- Formulated continuous exponential flare equations ($R = 0.16\text{ mm}^{-1}$) and integrated four wide curved parasitic side slits to suppress higher-order cross-polarization modes.
- Evaluated comparative dielectric performance ($\varepsilon_r = 3.6$ vs $\varepsilon_r = 2.2$) and modeled cylindrical aerodynamic conformal bending for UAV fuselage seeker pods.

### 2. **Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) UAV Altitude Control System via 915 MHz SDR Telemetry**
*Tools: ADALM-Pluto SDR, Python, PID Autopilot, FM Telemetry, IIR Filtering*
- Developed a real-time HIL simulation connecting a 2nd-order aircraft dynamics plant model with a closed-loop PID altitude control autopilot.
- Transmitted flight telemetry over a real 915 MHz RF link using an ADALM-Pluto SDR, performing quadrature demodulation and single-pole IIR noise filtering to reconstruct the control feedback loop under AWGN conditions.

### 3. **2.4 GHz 5th-Order Chebyshev Stepped-Impedance Low-Pass Filter**
*Tools: Keysight ADS, Keysight LineCalc, CST 3D EM, FR4 Microstrip*
- Synthesized and simulated a 5th-order distributed stepped-impedance microstrip LPF operating at a 2.4 GHz cutoff frequency for ISM-band avionics interference suppression.
- Performed Monte Carlo statistical yield analysis to model PCB substrate tolerance variations and validated 3D electromagnetic performance in CST.

### 4. **Two-Wheeled Inverted Pendulum (TWIP) Robot: LQR vs. Cascaded PID Analysis**
*Tools: Control Systems Theory, MPU6050 6-DOF IMU, C++ Embedded Firmware*
- Derived non-linear Lagrangian dynamic equations and state-space representations for an inherently open-loop unstable inverted pendulum robot.
- Built a physical robotic platform, implementing sensor fusion and cascaded PID control with anti-windup clamping to resolve practical actuator deadband and unobserved state limitations.

### 5. **CNC 2D Handwriting Machine & Vector Plotter**
*Tools: Arduino Uno, CNC Shield, GRBL v1.1, A4988 Drivers, NEMA 14 Steppers, CATIA*
- Engineered a dual-axis electro-mechanical drafting plotter with custom-modeled CAD gantry and sub-millimeter precision.
- Flashed and calibrated GRBL firmware for high-speed synchronized stepper pulse coordination over UART.

### 6. **Autonomous Competition Heavy Rover**
*Tools: ESP32 Dual-Core, Dual BTS7960 43A H-Bridges, JGA-25-370 Geared Motors, 4S2P Li-Ion Pack*
- Designed high-torque robotic rover with custom 16.8V battery management, high-amperage H-bridge motor drives, and real-time WiFi/BLE telemetry diagnostics.
