## Research & Engineering Projects

### **Super Wideband (3–18 GHz) Conformal Vivaldi Antenna for Electronic Warfare** | *CST Studio Suite, Rogers RT6035HTC, CPW Feed*
*Lead Designer — NUST CAE* | *Aug 2026*
- Designed and numerically simulated an ultra-compact planar and aerodynamically conformal Vivaldi antenna achieving a 6:1 impedance bandwidth ratio with return loss S11 < -10 dB across 3.0 to 18.0 GHz for airborne Electronic Support Measures (ESM).
- Synthesized a single-layer Co-Planar Waveguide (CPW) feed transition eliminating via inductance, and formulated an exponential flare profile (opening rate R = 0.16 mm⁻¹) with 4 curved ground slits to suppress destructive higher-order cross-polarization modes.
- Modeled aerodynamic conformal cylindrical curvature matching UAV fuselage contours and validated inter-element mutual coupling isolation (S21 < -25 dB).
